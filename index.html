Lantern Run Privacy Policy

Publisher: Lee Dell Bay Innovations LLC · Effective date: October 11, 2026

Lantern Run is a free game. You keep a lantern lit while you collect crystals in a night forest. This page explains what information the game itself handles, and what the advertising and purchase services it uses handle. The two are kept separate below.

1. Information the app collects

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location. There is no sign-in. The game does not use analytics or crash-reporting services.

The game stores these on your device: your level progress, stored light, settings and a saved game state. The game also uses an anonymous player ID so that purchases and light can be kept safe. When you first play online, our server creates a random player ID and a secret key for your device. Both are stored on your device. The server keeps, against that random ID:

  • the light balance and the level you have reached;
  • when you last used the Collect button, and which levels have paid their bonus;
  • counts of crystals and potions found, used to stop impossible claims;
  • your purchases: the product bought and a one-way hash of the purchase token (not your payment details), and whether ads were removed;
  • confirmations that you watched a rewarded ad.

This record is not linked to your name or email. Like any internet request, your requests reach our server with your IP address. The server uses it only to deliver the response, and it may appear in short-lived server logs (kept for 14 days).

2. Information Google AdMob may collect and process

Lantern Run shows ads from Google AdMob: a full-screen ad between some levels, and an optional "watch an ad" for extra light. When an ad is requested, Google's advertising SDK may collect and use:

  • your advertising ID (the Android Advertising ID, or the iOS IDFA if you allow tracking);
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and operating-system information, such as model, OS version, language and screen size;
  • how you interact with ads, and whether an ad was shown or completed.

Google uses this to choose and show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure results, and prevent fraud. Google acts as an independent controller for some of this data. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Advertising and personalised advertising

Ads may be personalised, meaning chosen using your advertising ID and your past activity. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another place where consent is required, the game shows a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalised. On iPhone and iPad, personalised ads need your permission through Apple's tracking prompt; if you say no, ads are not personalised. The game plays the same either way.

You can remove ads with the "Remove ads" purchase in the Shop. Paid builds on some platforms have no ads.

4. Device identifiers, IP address, approximate location and diagnostic data

  • The game itself uses a random player ID (section 1) and, on your device only, a device identifier to protect your saved game from tampering. That device identifier is not sent to us.
  • The advertising SDK uses the advertising ID and IP address as described in section 2.
  • The game does not read your GPS location. The real-world terrain in the levels is included in the app.
  • We do not collect crash reports or diagnostic data. Short-lived server logs may record that a request was made, when, and from which IP address.

5. How information is used

  • To run the game: keep your light balance, progress and purchases correct, and refuse impossible or repeated claims.
  • To deliver purchases and rewards you asked for.
  • By Google, to show and measure ads (section 2).

We do not sell your information and we do not use it to build profiles of you.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Data on your device stays until you use "Reset progress" in Settings or uninstall the app.
  • The anonymous server record is kept while it is in use so your purchases and light stay correct.
  • Server logs are kept for 14 days.
  • To have your server record deleted, email us at the address in section 12. The app does not show your player ID, so tell us roughly when you installed and what you purchased and we will help identify the record. Records we cannot identify cannot be linked back to you.

8. Children's privacy

Lantern Run is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are in a country with a higher age of digital consent, the higher age applies. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Requests between the game and our server are sent over HTTPS and signed with your device's secret key, and purchases are verified with Google or Apple on the server. No method is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect so there is little to protect.

10. User choices and advertising controls

  • Consent choices: if the game showed you a consent form, open Settings → Privacy choices to change your answer at any time.
  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads, to delete your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation.
  • iPhone and iPad: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, to allow or refuse tracking for Lantern Run.
  • Google: Google Ad Settings controls personalised ads across Google services.
  • Remove ads: available in the Shop.

11. Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, we will update this page and the effective date above.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to leedellbay@gmail.com.
